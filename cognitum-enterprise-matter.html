The solution-design method Begin with the conditions — not the atoms

1
Define the condition — what's failing, expensive, slow, risky, or impossible today?
2
Define what must exist — which decision, behavior, or capability resolves it?
3
Select atoms — which Cognitum capabilities provide the functions?
4
Design bonds — APIs, MCP, events, data, identity, policy, verification, RuFlo.
5
Name the molecule — one coherent solution, not a list of technologies.
6
Place it into matter — how it works with the enterprise's other solutions.
7
Prove the outcome — baseline, acceptance threshold, safety boundary, scale path, economics.

Consulting rule: without a named buyer, integration owner, acceptance threshold, and scale path, it's a demonstration — not a commercial solution.

Market prioritization

Tier 1 · buildings, factories, warehouses
Clear physical signals & integration surfaces · measurable energy/downtime/throughput/alert economics · strong direct-enterprise + OEM demand · lower claims burden.
Tier 2 · care environments
Strong privacy/latency/workflow/staffing rationale · start with adjunct workflow support, not diagnosis · requires clinical validation, quality systems, careful claims.
Tier 3 · regulated knowledge ops
Large but crowded · win where local operation, evidence, existing data estates & specialized workflows beat generic chat.
Lead with Tier 1

Measurable economics and a lower claims burden make buildings, factories, and warehouses the cleanest first molecules to sell.
