07 @metaharness/darwin · the engine

08 Darwin · the loop & the gate

One generation, four steps

1
Mutate

Change exactly one surface → a child variant.

2
Sandbox

Shell-free, secrets scrubbed from the environment.

3
Score

Six 0–1 terms, minus penalties for unsafe or wasteful behaviour.

4
Promote

Replace the parent only if every gate holds.

The promotion gate — all four must hold

  • Score improves over the parent by the delta.
  • Safety ≥ 0.95 on an independent check.
  • No test regression — pass rate never drops.
  • Zero blocked actions during the run.
Two independent safety checks

inspectVariant() blocks unapproved files, symlinks, and dangerous patterns; validateGeneratedCode() gates LLM code before it touches disk. Penalties bite: secret exposure −0.30, destructive −0.25.

Why the numbers move

Open-loop resolves 7.7%. A closed-loop repair harness roughly doubles it to 15.3%. Tiered escalation — cheap first, frontier only when needed — reaches 58.3%, at ~$0.01–$0.74 per instance versus $1–20 frontier-only.

The weights never changed. The harness did.

11 What you hand a skeptic

  • A lift curve — improvement you can see, not assert.
  • A promotion lineage — the chain of winners, and why each passed.
  • A replay bundle — reproduce it on another machine.
  • Signed receipts — Ed25519 proof the run is the run.
