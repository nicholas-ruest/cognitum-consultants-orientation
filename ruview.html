01.1 The problem with today's sensors

Every existing option makes you trade something away

  • Cameras — invasive, GDPR-heavy, blind in the dark.
  • PIR motion sensors — short range, line-of-sight only, no vitals.
  • Wearables — a compliance burden; people take them off.
  • Wi-Fi is already there — and it reflects off everything, including you.
What each sensor gives up

Your body bends the Wi-Fi in the room. RuView reads the bend.

the RuView idea

Every person perturbs the multipath of ambient Wi-Fi. Those perturbations live in Channel State Information — and cheap ESP32 radios can capture them, through walls and in the dark, without ever forming an image.

Signal in · perception out

01.3 What makes it work — CSI

Channel State Information is the raw material

  • 56 subcarriers per channel — fine-grained amplitude & phase.
  • Body-induced phase shifts — motion, breathing, a heartbeat.
  • 3 channels fused — multi-band for a richer picture.
  • Commodity radios — an ESP32 captures it for a few dollars.

  • Heart rate — real-time, 40–120 BPM, from chest micro-motion.

03.3 Pose & activity

Pose — a skeleton from radio

  • 17 keypoints tracked from Wi-Fi alone.
  • 82.69% torso-PCK@20 on MM-Fi (single model).
  • 83.59% with a 3-model ensemble + test-time augmentation.

Activity — and falls

  • Walking, sitting, gestures recognized in real time.
  • Fall detection <200 ms — 3-frame debounce + 5 s cooldown.
  • Adaptive normalization for multi-person scenes.
Honest about maturity

The MM-Fi and encoder models are validated; the on-device pose model is labeled weak (PCK@20 = 3.0%) and shipped as a runtime stub — RuView states this plainly rather than hiding it.

PART 04

Under the hood

The signal chain that turns noisy CSI into a clean skeleton.

fusionDSPself-learningprivacy gate

04.1 Signal processing

Classic DSP does the heavy lifting before the net

  • Hampel filter — reject impulsive outliers in the CSI.
  • SpotFi — angle-of-arrival & time-of-flight super-resolution.
  • Fresnel model — map phase to physical motion zones.
  • BVP — body-coordinate velocity profiles for activity.
Noisy CSI → clean signal

04.2 Multi-band & multistatic fusion

More viewpoints, more certainty

  • Multi-band — 3 channels × 56 subcarriers fused per node.
  • Multistatic — N×(N-1) cross-viewpoint links between nodes.
  • Coherence gate — drop links that don't agree, keep the signal.
  • Adaptive dedup — runtime-tunable person counting.
Every node sees every other

04.3 The self-learning system · ADR-024

It learns the room without a single label

  • Contrastive embeddings — trained on 12.2M steps / 60K frames.
  • Room fingerprints — the model learns each space's signature.
  • Anomaly aware — flags what doesn't fit the learned baseline.
  • Self-supervised — no annotation, no camera ground truth.
Contrastive learning · no labels

04.4 Privacy by construction · BFLD

Sense the body, never the identity

  • Beamforming Feedback Layer — a privacy-gated signal path.
  • Structural gate — identity leakage is prevented by design, not policy.
  • Measured — identity matching is "not separable on Wi-Fi-only channels."
  • No image ever formed — presence and pose, without a face.
Pose passes · identity blocked

05.2 Bring up a sensor

Flash an ESP32, point it at your Wi-Fi

  • Flash firmware — the Rust CSI node over USB.
  • Provision — hand it your SSID and password.
  • It joins the mesh — and starts streaming CSI.
  • Python or Rust — pip install ruview or the crates.
# flash the CSI node
python -m esptool --chip esp32s3 \
  --port COM9 write_flash ...

# provision Wi-Fi
python provision.py --port COM9 \
  --ssid "WiFi" --password "secret"

pip install "ruview[client]"
