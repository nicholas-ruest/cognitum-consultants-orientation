# promotions-campaign

Turn one chosen bet into a promotion you can actually measure.

1. Analyst pulls the baseline for the KPI this promotion must move (conversion, AOV, retention, pipeline — whatever the bet targets).
2. Strategist confirms the bet and the trade-off it accepts, so the promotion isn't chasing a metric the business isn't betting on.
3. Promoter designs the promotion: the offer, the audience, the channels, the message, the timing, and a spend ceiling.
4. Output a one-page brief: offer, audience, channels, primary KPI + baseline, 7-day read, kill-switch metric, budget ceiling, owner.

The brief is a proposal. Sending, publishing, and spending stay with the user.
