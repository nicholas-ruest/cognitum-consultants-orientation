  • ONE — Operating Network for Enterprise: the umbrella offer & naming anchor

  • Below the anchors, exactly 100 numbered modules: 10 overlay categories + 90 supporting components
  • Grouped into four colour-coded acts — the operating platform, trust & data, build & infrastructure, and running the business
  • Two modules fit into almost any discussion — ONE META and ONE CLOUD — because nearly every enterprise is already spending heavily on LLM tokens and cloud
